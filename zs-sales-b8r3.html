Discovery Call — Full Script

20 minutes · works for Zoom, phone, or in-person coffee

▶
This isn't a sales pitch — it's a conversation. The goal is to listen, understand their problem, and then show them Sunblock Studio can solve it. Let them talk more than Sunblock Studio does in the first half.
Phase 1 — Opener (2 min)

Warm up, set the tone

You say

Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time — we'll keep this to about 20 minutes. We just want to learn a bit about your business and see if we can actually help. Sound good?

You say

Tell us about what you do — like give us the version you'd tell someone at a dinner party, not the formal pitch.

→ Let them talk. Take notes. Don't interrupt. This gives the context needed to position the pitch.

Phase 2 — Dig in (8 min)

Find the pain, understand the gap

You say

Do you have a website right now? What does it look like?

You say

When someone Googles you or asks for your site — what happens? Do you feel good sending people there?

You say

Where are most of your clients coming from right now — word of mouth, social media, search?

You say

What's your biggest frustration with how you're showing up online right now?

You say

If your website was doing its job perfectly — what would that look like for you? More calls? More bookings? More trust when people look you up?

→ The last question is the most important. Their answer is literally what your proposal will say you're going to deliver.

Phase 3 — The pitch (5 min)

Position what you do, match it to their pain

You say

Here's what we do — we build custom websites for businesses like yours in about 7 days. Not templates, not DIY builders. A real site designed to look professional and actually bring in clients.

You say

Based on what you just told me, it sounds like what you need is [repeat back their answer from the last question]. That's exactly what we'd build for you.

You say

We have two packages. The Essential — 5 pages, done in 7 days, $750. Or the Professional — 8 pages, SEO, booking system, Google Business setup, $1,200. From what you've described, we'd probably recommend [package] because [reason from their answers].

→ Always recommend one. Do not make them choose without guidance — it creates hesitation.

Phase 4 — Close (5 min)

Handle questions, get a yes or a next step

You say

Does that sound like what you're looking for? Any questions about what's included?

You say — if they seem ready

Awesome. What we'll do is send you a proposal today with everything written out, plus the contract and deposit link. Once the deposit is in, we can start within 24 hours — and you'd have a live site in 7 days.

You say — if they need to think

Totally fine. We'll send the proposal over and you can look it over. The only thing we'd say is we can only take on [X] clients at a time, so if you want to lock in the timeline it's worth moving soon. No pressure though — what's a good timeframe for you?
Common objections

We already have a website

That's fine — can we take a quick look? A lot of the sites we rebuild were technically "there" but weren't doing anything for the business. If yours is working great, we'll tell you honestly. If not, we can show you exactly what we'd change.

That's more than we expected to spend

We get it. If a site brings in even one or two extra clients a month, it pays for itself in 30 days. Sunblock Studio also has the Essential package at $750 — same quality, just fewer pages. That might be the right starting point.

Can we think about it?

Of course. We'll send the proposal over so you have everything in writing. What's your timeline looking like — are you trying to get this done in the next few weeks or is it more of a someday thing?

Can you do it cheaper?

The pricing is set — we keep it fixed because it's what lets us deliver a quality product on a fast timeline. What we can do is make sure you're on the right package so you're not paying for things you don't need.

How do we know it'll look good?

Fair question — before we start, we can send a private preview packet or a visual direction board so you can see the style we would use. You will also review the first draft before anything goes live.

In-Person Cheat Sheet

When you run into someone — networking, coffee, referral

▶
You have 60 seconds. Don't pitch — just ask questions and plant a seed. The goal is a follow-up, not a sale on the spot.
The one-liner

When someone asks what you do

We build websites for small businesses in Miami — ones that actually bring in clients, not just look pretty. We do it in about a week.
The follow-up question

Immediately after

What do you do? Do you have a site?

→ If they say no, or they say yes but seem hesitant: "We'd love to take a look sometime — we can usually spot pretty quickly what's costing someone business." Then exchange contacts.

The next step

Before you part ways

We'll send over a private preview packet or design direction board — just so you have something to look at. What's the best way to reach you, text or Instagram?

→ Get their number or handle. Follow up within 2 hours while you're still fresh in their mind.

Project Scope

Studio agrees to design and develop a custom website for Client under the [Essential / Professional] Package, which includes:

[List deliverables from the chosen package — e.g. 5 pages, contact form, mobile responsive design, domain setup]

Any work outside this scope will require a separate written agreement and additional payment.

Timeline

Studio will deliver a design draft within 2–3 business days of receiving the deposit and all required assets from Client. Final site will be launched within [7 / 10] business days of project start, provided Client supplies all requested content (logo, photos, copy) within 48 hours of request.

Delays caused by Client's failure to provide materials will extend the timeline accordingly.

Payment

Total project fee: $[750 / 1,200]

• 50% deposit ($[375 / 600]) is due upon signing this agreement. Work begins only after deposit is received.
• 50% balance ($[375 / 600]) is due upon completion, before the site is launched and transferred.

All payments are processed via Stripe. Deposit is non-refundable once work has begun.

Revisions

This agreement includes 2 rounds of revisions. A revision round is defined as a single consolidated list of changes submitted by Client. Additional revision rounds beyond the 2 included will be billed at $75/hour.

Revisions must be submitted within 48 hours of each draft delivery. Delays beyond 48 hours may affect the project timeline.

Ownership & Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of final payment, Client owns the completed website and all custom design files created for this project.

Studio may request permission to reference the completed work in marketing materials after launch. Client can decline that request in writing.

Any third-party assets (fonts, stock photos, plugins) remain subject to their respective licenses.

Ongoing Care Plans (Optional)

Client may opt into a monthly Care plan after launch:

• Care Basic — $79/mo: Hosting, daily backups, uptime monitoring, security updates.
• Care Pro — $149/mo: Everything in Basic, plus 2 hours of edits/month, monthly Google Business post, monthly ranking report, priority support, and 1 free page per quarter.

Care plans are billed monthly via Stripe and may be cancelled with 30 days written notice. If Client does not maintain a Care plan, Studio is not responsible for hosting, maintenance, or security of the site.

Cancellation

If Client cancels after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable. If Studio cancels for any reason other than Client's breach of this agreement, Studio will refund the deposit in full.

Either party may terminate this agreement with written notice if the other party materially breaches its terms and fails to remedy the breach within 5 business days.

Studio's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by Client. Studio is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use or inability to use the delivered website.
